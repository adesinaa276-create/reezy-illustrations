Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Here are answers to some of the most common inquiries about booking a session with Reezy Illustrations.

How do I book a photography session?

Simply fill out the contact form below or reach out through WhatsApp or Instagram. I'll respond within 24 hours to discuss your session.

How long does it take to receive my photos?

Depending on the package, your professionally edited gallery will be delivered within 5–10 business days.

Do you travel for photoshoots?

Yes. I travel for weddings, events, graduations, and destination sessions. Additional travel charges may apply for locations outside Osun State.

What should I wear to my session?

Once your booking is confirmed, I'll provide outfit recommendations and styling tips before your session.

Can I reschedule my booking?

Yes. Sessions can be rescheduled if you notify me at least 48 hours before your scheduled date.

Are all images professionally edited?

Absolutely. Every package includes professionally edited, high-resolution images ready for printing and sharing.
